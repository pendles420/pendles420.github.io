about

Chris Carroll (born 2000) is a Brooklyn, New York based artist who converts lived experience into art. He is interested in reaching the inside through first politely accessing the outside. He is becoming increasingly suspicious that there is no inside. Just an outside. Or at least, the outside and the inside are no different. He has several short stories published on various publications and has a book of sentences coming out soon within the next two years. He has two hands. In 2019, he screamed extremely loud. So loud, that it killed an old person's life. Thankfully the old person was okay. It was just their life that died.
